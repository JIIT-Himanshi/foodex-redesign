# Foodex India

Foodex India is a modern food and snacks brand website designed to showcase premium Indian namkeen, sweets, gift packs, cookies, and ready-to-eat products through a clean, visually rich, and user-friendly experience.

The project focuses on creating a modern digital presence for Foodex India by combining premium product presentation, smooth interactions, responsive layouts, and thoughtful user experience design.

## Features

### Hero Section

* Premium split-layout hero design
* Featured product showcase
* Floating product animation
* Responsive typography and call-to-action buttons

### Brand Trust Strip

* Highlights key company achievements
* Established year, product range, retail reach, and market presence
* Clean and minimal presentation

### Featured Categories

* Premium category cards
* Product-focused visuals with transparent PNG assets
* Hover interactions and subtle animations
* Fully responsive layout

### Best Sellers

* Curated collection of Foodex's most popular products
* Product cards with pricing information
* Interactive hover effects
* Product spotlight section

### Featured Product Spotlight

* Dedicated showcase for a flagship product
* Front and back product packaging view
* Interactive flip animation
* Premium presentation with depth, shadow, and motion effects

### Retail & Distribution Partners

* Trusted partner showcase
* Continuous marquee animation
* Includes major retail and delivery platforms

### Foodex Difference

* Highlights brand strengths and product quality
* Premium ingredients
* Traditional recipes
* Quality assurance
* Trusted manufacturing practices

### Newsletter Section

* Email subscription component
* Clean and conversion-focused design

### Footer

* Brand information
* Quick navigation links
* Copyright information
* Designer credit
