# Foodex India Redesign

> **A modern React-based redesign of the Foodex India website, created to deliver a premium, responsive, and business-focused digital experience through improved UI/UX, interactive product discovery, reusable architecture, and smooth user interactions.**

The project reimagines the existing Foodex India website while preserving the brand's identity and traditional FMCG presence. It combines a modern visual system with product discovery, business information, distributor engagement, corporate storytelling, and shopping-oriented functionality.

---

# Project Overview

Foodex India is an FMCG brand offering a range of namkeen, sweets, cookies, bakery products, and ready-to-eat food products.

The redesign focuses on creating a stronger and more modern digital presence through:

- Premium and Brand-Consistent UI/UX
- Responsive Design Across Devices
- Improved Product Discovery
- Interactive Shopping Experience
- Business and Distributor Engagement
- Corporate Brand Storytelling
- Reusable React Architecture
- Smooth Animations and Micro-interactions
- Light and Dark Theme Support
